
# Welcome to SUGSI! 

Hey everyone! I’m **SUGSI** (also known as **Blaze!**).

I wanted to share a quick update on my development journey. I’ve officially moved on from custom ROM development. Because with the constant accusations of copying other ROMs and renaming them was getting tired. On top of that, my thing always and always been to **never release a ROM with known bugs**. In my experience, releasing unfinished work leads to poor user experiences and fewer downloads.

Because of that, I’ve shifted my focus entirely to **GSIs**!

### What does SUGSI mean?

**SUGSI** stands for **StrataUI GSIs**.

### My Journey & Device History 📱

* **Samsung Galaxy A51 (Exynos 9611):** This was where my first community started! Unfortunately, after the screen broke twice and repairs weren't easy and expensive, I had to mark the project as *EOL (End of Life)*.
* **Samsung Galaxy J7:** My backup device. I created tons of ports and GSIs here until the screen eventually gave out as well.
* **Samsung Galaxy A32:** This became my main phone! I successfully ported and ran **ColorOS**, **One UI**, **FlymeOS**, **OriginOS**, **Nothing OS**, and even **postmarketOS**.
* **The Shift:** Since the A32 is now my daily driver, I can't keep flashing experimental ports on it. That’s what inspired me to pivot into building and maintaining reliable GSIs for the community full-time!

### 🌟 First Release

My very first official GSI build is **PixelOS 17**!

A huge thank you to **MysticGSI** for allowing me to use some of their patches to make this project possible.

Stay tuned for more updates, and thank you all for the support! 🙌
